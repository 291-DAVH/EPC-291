# Product vision

## Statement
For engineering offices and EPC contractors that must deliver consistent, traceable engineering documents on time, **EPC-291** is a platform that automates the repetitive part of an EPC project (calculation, drawings, specifications, bill of materials, quotations, review and audit packages) while keeping the engineer in charge of every decision and signature. Unlike spreadsheets and generic project tools, it turns the customer's own words into verifiable requirements, checks work against the applicable standards, and delivers only controlled, versioned documents.

## Users
| User | Need | Sees |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering office staff (end user) | Know the state of every project, receive deliverables, send client information | Portal: projects, deliverables, input data, requirements, reports |
| Project engineer / discipline engineer | Produce and check engineering with less rework | Workspace, review packages |
| Reviewer / auditor | Independent checks with evidence | Review and audit packages |
| Administrator (us / the customer's admin) | Operate the platform | Technical console |
| Client of the office | Be understood and receive documents | Deliverables and reports through the office |

## Value
1. Hours saved on calculation, drawings and documents.
2. Consistency across documents (one source per datum) and traceability from requirement to deliverable.
3. Compliance with the standards of each country, checked by rules extracted from them.
4. Customer input turned into structured data without manual transcription.

## Scope of version 1
Argentina; electrical low voltage; PM2 methodology (PMI selectable later); web portal; single-organization use first, subscriptions after.

## Non-goals (for now)
Replacing the engineer's responsibility or signature; CAD authoring (we generate DXF, the engineer draws what remains); 3D/BIM; financial management (client prices and budgets are confidential and never stored).

## Success measures
- Time to produce a complete LV package (calculation, SLD, specification, BOM) versus manual baseline.
- Share of requirements traced to a deliverable.
- Rules verified per country, and findings caught before audit.
- Portal task completion without help.

## Principles
Engineer decides, platform prepares. No invented technical data. Private material never leaves local. The client sees deliverables and data, not machinery.
