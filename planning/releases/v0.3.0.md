# v0.3.0 (2026-10-24)

Stories delivered since v0.2.0: US-0016 (automatic analysis of customer input), US-0018 (authentication and organizations), US-0019 (multi-line diagram generator).

## Highlights
- **Accounts and organizations:** real sign-in (email or alias, and Google, GitHub, Facebook, LinkedIn), individual and corporate subscriptions with seats, company owner / administrator / member, invitations, tenant isolation, one expert staff account that can work inside any organization, platform audit log, security headers.
- **Roles and permissions:** a permission matrix enforced by the server; norm reviewer; inspector and external auditor that see only the organizations assigned to them; observations from inspections and audits that the customer answers.
- **Engineering to delivery:** master data (company, clients, people, logos), issuing with stamped DXF, PDF, transmittal document, ZIP package and the email text; design criteria that govern the calculation and feed the single-line and multi-line diagrams (plugin `board-design`).
- **Management:** reports D11 to D15 of the PM² methodology generated from the project's data, registers (risks, changes, issues, decisions, assumptions, stakeholders, observations) defined by the methodology, answers to the customer's open questions with author and time.
- **Norm review:** every rule, table and design criterion can be corrected and approved point by point, with review state and log; the 15 base design criteria were reviewed and approved by the maintainer.
- **Single source of truth:** a gate check against duplicated files, one catalog of design criteria, one library of symbols; a gate check that keeps any development credential out of the repository.
- **Quality:** 23-check battery; identity tests also run on PostgreSQL in CI.

## Known limits
- Sign-in with Google, GitHub, Facebook and LinkedIn is tested with simulated providers only: it must be tried once with real keys (`docs/oauth-providers.md`).
- No email delivery (invitation links and the message of a delivery are copied by hand), no password recovery by email, no two-factor authentication.
- The rules of the calculation engine and those of the rules engine are still two representations (ADR-0012); calculation tables and most rules are pending approval in the norm review.
- Only the PM² methodology has registers and reports; PMI is not available.
- No license chosen (all rights reserved). Desktop (Rust) and FreeCAD/3D are deferred.
