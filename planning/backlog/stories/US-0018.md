# US-0018: Authentication and organizations

As the owner of the platform, I want this so that the product moves toward EP-007.

## Acceptance criteria
- Login (sessions, cookie or bearer token), logout, password change, sign-in throttling
- Organizations and roles: individual and corporate subscriptions (a group of people with seats), owner/member, platform staff; invitations; owner manages the people
- Tenant isolation tested (a customer reads, writes and lists only the projects of their organization)
