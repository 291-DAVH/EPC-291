# Changelog

All notable changes. Format: Keep a Changelog; versions follow `docs/BRANCHING.md` (releases are tagged on `main`).

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed
- Reading the walls of a vector PDF no longer fails with "the PDF has too many strokes to read" on a plan with hatching, ticks and furniture: over 60,000 strokes the door swings and the longest strokes are read and a warning says how many of the shortest were left out. Only a PDF beyond 1,500,000 strokes is refused, now saying how to export it lighter. A plan of 200,000 strokes is read in about 2 seconds.

### Added
- Final phase of a project: the new-project form has an **ending phase** next to the starting one (it cannot be before it; the default is as-built), because not every project goes through detail engineering or as-built. Both lists show the full names (Conceptual engineering, Basic engineering, Detail engineering, Execution engineering, As-built) instead of IC / IB / ID / IE, and as-built is now a phase the project can start or end at. The end phase is kept with the project and shown beside the current phase; the management workflow does not use it yet.
- In the circuit form, "Project criteria" now shows the value it stands for in brackets (for example "XLPE", "B2 · Multicore cable in conduit") and a note says where to change the default (Engineering → Design criteria).
- Circuits of a board, installation and cable: each circuit now says its **method of installation** (B1, B2, C, E, F, G, D1, D2 of the tables, with a plain description), its **insulation** (PVC / XLPE) and its **conductor** (Cu / Al); empty means the design criteria of the project. The calculation follows them (a 166 A circuit needs 95 mm² in a conduit and 70 mm² on a ladder tray). The engine's lists are the only source; values it does not know are rejected on save.
- Circuits table, compact: once accepted, a circuit is a single row (tag, description, load, length, installation and the whole calculation) with two small buttons, ✎ edit and ✕ remove. The data form is shown under the table only while a circuit is added or edited, and goes away on Accept (Enter) or Cancel (Escape); accepting or removing calculates again.
- One table for the circuits of a board (Engineering → Technical data): each circuit's data and its calculation (type, Ib, In, Icu, section, ΔU, limit, protection, compliance) in the same row, and the groups the standard solutions add listed under them. "+ Add circuit" opens a row at the end of the table: Enter or "Accept" adds the circuit, saves the board and calculates it at once (sections, protections, drops), and "Calculate" does the same for edited rows. The calculation runs alone (new `diagrams=calc` mode of `epc_drawing.board_design`): it does not make the memo or the diagrams, keeps the ones of an earlier run (the memo shows as out of date until it is run again) and the sizing of the automatic design uses it too.
- Automatic design after the luminaires are accepted: applying the luminaires to the plan makes the memo of the study (as before) and then walks the person through the route of the conduits and trays (shown on the plan, accepted with a click), the circuits over that route (created in the board once accepted) and the sizing: each circuit's cable is calculated in its board with the length the plan measured (the height of the plenums it crosses included) and each conduit or tray gets the smallest commercial size that holds its cables within the allowed fill (`epc_plan.sizing`, typical cable diameters and sizes in `params.json`, to confirm against the project's catalogue). A detail popup lists cables, plenums, sizes and fills and flags what needs a look; approving writes the sizes on the plan lines. New endpoint `POST /layout/{level}/design/sizing`; the engine's stretch containers now carry their line id.
- Choosing the board you are adding: placing a board node on an Electrical plan always lists the boards of the project not yet on it (name, level and place, with a warning when the board belongs to another level) and the one chosen is tied to the node; any load can also be linked to any board of the project from its properties ("Fed from the board"), so its circuits stay under that board or another.
- The board and the plenum of the building: adding the first Electrical sheet asks the name of the board, where it is and on which level (L-1, L0...), makes the levels the board needs and the electrical sheet on its own level (a board in the basement is no longer drawn on L0); the board keeps its level and its place. Adding a level asks for the plenum (vertical shaft) of the level of the board when none crosses it, and the level waits for the answer; the plenum says its code, its place, the height it adds and how far it goes (all the levels or a number up and down), and its node `PL` is put, at the same place, in the electrical sheet of each level in range, also of the levels made afterwards and of the plans that are calibrated later. The other disciplines that have a kind of vertical shaft are asked whether they need one (the electrical one is the only profile with a shaft today).
- PDF reader as a shared piece of the interface (`PdfViewer` and `usePdfUrl` in `@epc/ui`): any screen gives the path of a PDF in the API and gets the viewer, a way to open it in another tab and to download it. It fetches the file with the session and shows it from a local copy, so the server's rule that forbids framing its pages (`X-Frame-Options: DENY`) no longer blanks it. Engineering → Files uses it for the lighting report.
- Project screens reorganised: the drawings (was "Trazado") are now **Planos**, a section of **Ingeniería** that holds every discipline over the plan; "Datos del unifilar" is **Información técnica**; the "Plan" tab is **Gestión** and holds the registers (Registros) as a section. Old links to the drawings and the registers keep working. The scale is calibrated **only on the Architecture sheet** of a level and travels with the plan to every other discipline (a scale stored by another discipline is ignored; the editor has no scale tool outside Architecture). The illuminance map writes its lux over the plan, thinning out as it is zoomed out so that it never crowds. The lighting report is made with the study (no button needed) and is listed, with a viewer, in Ingeniería → Archivos: Engineering files now include PDF documents.
- Lighting improvements (the backlog of `docs/BACKLOG_ILUMINACION.md`): check the plan as it is drawn (each room calculated with the luminaires that are on it, wherever they were moved); compare the luminaires of the catalogue for a room (best first) and choose the luminaire of the rooms that have none; energy against the person's own limit of W/m², with hours and tariff (the engine has none, so nothing is invented); power factor per luminaire, used by the design of the circuits (apparent power); copy the parameters of a room to others, templates, activity suggested from the name of the room; lines of equal illuminance with their lux and export of the map to PNG; the sheet remembers its options; the error of the study is shown in the panel. Catalogue: duplicate, search, groups, favourites, power factor and import from a spreadsheet. Emergency luminaires are applied to the plan as nodes `LE` with their own family of circuit (`IUE`), and recalculating keeps the circuit of what it replaces. The report has the title block of the project, the energy table and the plan of the sheet with the luminaires and the illuminance of its rooms (the PDF exporter gained a `figure` block).
- Decreto 351/79 as a norm package of lighting (ADR-0030, phase 7): its rules are a unit of the norms review ("Lighting levels"): the reviewer corrects a value, its unit, clause or summary and approves it; approving marks the rule for the engine, and a value changed afterwards by other means shows as stale. A room can take its target from an approved rule (`target_rule` in the engine, "Level from the norm" in the Lighting panel): the target is shown with its norm and clause, a pending rule is offered but cannot be chosen, and a study goes stale when the rule it used changes. The rules that are not levels in lux (uniformity, luminance ratios) are reviewed but not used yet. Nothing is approved by default: the values are pending the owner's review.
- Emergency lighting and UGR in the lighting engine (ADR-0030, phase 6): `epc_lighting` ports from `epc_aea90364.lighting` the antipanic emergency lighting (the smallest regular grid that keeps the minimum illuminance and the max/min ratio on the floor, with escape routes checked along their axis), the UGR comparison (the value comes from photometric software; `not_verifiable` without it) and the flux-versus-direct check, with the same worked cases. The document takes an `emergency` list and `ugr_calculated` in a room; the result has `emergency`, `ugr` and `verification.direct_check`. In the Lighting panel a room can have an emergency luminaire, shown over the plan as marks (not drawn as nodes yet: the electrical profile has no emergency luminaire kind) and listed in the report. The old module `epc_aea90364.lighting` and its tests are removed (ADR-0028, phase 3).
- Exterior areas and illuminance map (ADR-0030, phase 5): a room marked "Exterior area" in the Lighting panel is calculated as floodlights from the real area of its outline (any polygon), its target and an exterior luminaire (the engine's beam method; the count is calculated, where the poles go is the designer's; the target must be given because no table of typical exterior values ships yet). "Illuminance map" draws over the plan the direct illuminance of every cell of each room, with its scale; for that the engine gives the grid (`Options.return_grid`) and the interior luminaires of the factory list carry an analytic intensity curve (a cosine power law that emits all the lamp flux, flagged illustrative). Cells outside the outline of a room that is not a rectangle are left out. The report lists the exterior areas.
- Lighting study, catalogue screens and report (ADR-0030, phase 4): applying the luminaires keeps the calculation as the study of the sheet (`src/lighting.json`, with a fingerprint of the rooms, their parameters, the scale, the options and the luminaires used); the panel shows it as up to date or out of date when any of them changes. "Make the report" renders a PDF memo (`LIGHTING_<sheet>.pdf`, Spanish or English) among the engineering documents, so the issuing flow offers it as a deliverable; a stale study has no report. The catalogue of luminaires has its screen in Data (company) and in the project's Catalogs. The PDF render of structured reports moved to `epc.core.engine.render_report_pdf`.
- Apply the luminaires to the plan (ADR-0030, phase 3): "Apply to the plan" in the Lighting panel draws one luminaire node (`LU`, with its tag, room, luminaire and power in `attrs.lighting`) per placement; calculating and applying again replaces what a calculation placed in that room and leaves what the person drew. The automatic design of circuits now adds the power each luminaire says of itself (the typical value of the family is only used for loads that say nothing). In a room that is not a rectangle, the luminaires of the bounding box that fall outside its real outline are not placed, and a warning says so.
- Lighting panel in the plan editor (ADR-0030, phase 2): on an electrical sheet, a "Lighting" tab on the right rail lists the rooms; for each one the person picks activity, luminaire, method, height, target and utilisation (saved with the room), calculates, and sees per room whether it meets the target, how many luminaires are needed, spacing, power and what was estimated with little confidence. The luminaires show over the plan as dashed circles until the person decides; nothing is drawn yet (phase 3).
- Lighting in the backend (ADR-0030, phase 1): new module `lighting` that calculates luminaires for the rooms of a sheet with `epc_lighting` (`POST /api/projects/{client}/{code}/lighting/calc`, a proposal: nothing is saved), the geometry between the editor (view units, any outline) and the engine (metres, rectangles; a room that is not a rectangle uses its bounding box and says so), the choices for the panel (`/options`) and a catalogue of luminaires at three levels (factory in the engine, company at `/api/data/luminaires`, project at `/lighting/luminaires`; each stores only its changes).
- New engine `epc_lighting` (ADR-0029), handed over from `incoming/`: luminaire count (lumen and zonal-cavity methods), uniform layout with spacing check, point-by-point illuminance, exterior floodlights, power and energy, with every estimate carrying its reason and confidence. Standard library only; 158 tests. The norm data it carries (D351-79, 23 rules) is pending review and not used by the calculation.
- One engine per norm (ADR-0027): `engine/calc` is now `engine/aea90364` (package `epc_aea90364`) and holds everything of the AEA 90364 norm: calculation, tables, rules, design criteria, review state and promoted standards. A jurisdiction only says which norm engine it uses (`packs` in `jurisdiction.json`); `epc_standards.promote_rules` and `RuleSet` work on the norm engines, and the backend and the drawing engine ask `epc_aea90364.norm_data`. Environments that had `epc-calc` installed editable need `pip install -e engine/aea90364`.
- Layers panel and architecture: the panel lists only the architecture layers and those of the discipline being drawn (the other disciplines of the level keep their footprint switch and a way to open their sheet); the architectural plan is one per level, shared by every discipline drawn over it, and its picture is the layer ARQ-FONDO. HVAC, Fire protection and Communications now have drawing tools (kinds, layers and symbols).
- Automatic design in two steps (ADR-0026): first the runs, previewed on the plan (the fewest straight trunks possible, no bends, one spine from the board, a short conduit to each load) and accepted; then the wiring over those runs (one circuit per room and family of load, at most 15 outlets for lighting), checked with the engine and created in the board with their power in VA. Limits and powers are typical values in `params.json`, editable in the panel. The layers of the catalog have a tab per discipline in the layout and in the console.
- Architecture recognition (ADR-0025): a new isolated engine `epc_arch` reads a plan given as a DXF, a vector PDF or an image and finds its walls, doors and windows (with confidence and reason), as JSON and as an SVG with layers. One reader per source over the same primitives; the recognizers do not know files. The layout editor has a button to recognize the plan of a sheet, shows what was found, snaps to the faces and corners of the walls and runs lines parallel to them, and offers the scale guessed from the doors. One place renders a PDF page (the backend no longer has its own copy).
- Coordination between disciplines that stay separate (ADR-0024): a level holds one sheet per discipline (`L0`, `L0@sanitaria_cloacas`); each discipline issues only the footprint of what it occupies (position, width, heights, free space); clashes between disciplines (hard, clearance, maintenance space) are found on the issued versions, with rules as data at engine, company and project level, accepted with a reason on record; threads (comment, request, opening, reserved space) instead of editing another's drawing; data with one owner and the matrix of who gives what to whom; who edits each discipline; a view of the disciplines of a level together. New tab Coordinación and a panel in the layout editor.
- Layout editor tools: snapping (nodes, ends, midpoints, grid, vertices of a vector underlay) and orthogonal lock, continuous lines and typed lengths, selection by box and by crossing, copy/paste/duplicate/array and templates of the organization, copy to other levels, insert node on a line, align/distribute/rotate/mirror/scale, locks, measuring (distance, angle, area), route along the drawn network, context menu, command palette (Ctrl+K), configurable keys, minimap, view panel (layers, kinds, colour by circuit, lengths, isolate a circuit, underlay opacity/align/crop), coordinates bar, tooltips, alignment guides, history with compare and restore, a list of what changed since the last files, checks before making files, review mode, drafts kept until the server has them, PNG and print, pinch and two-finger move on touch.
- Plan engine: footprint and clash modules, how full a tray or conduit is (typical limits in `params.json`), the turn of a symbol reaches the DXF, shafts rise through the levels.
- The catalog of layers and the document of standard solutions have a company level and a project level (default < global < company < project). Each level stores only what it changes; the company edits them in the tab Datos and each project in the new tab Catálogos; the engine receives the files in order and the memo of a board is stale when the solutions in force for its project change. Changes to layers and solutions at any level are recorded in the audit log.

### Changed
- Layout (Trazado) is a single-page editor: a slim header, a top bar (levels, discipline, background plan, DXF version, generate), a vertical toolbox of icon buttons, a floating palette for the type of node or line, the canvas filling the window, and a right panel with tabs (properties, circuits, schedule, warnings). Picking something on the canvas brings its properties forward. On a phone the toolbox is a strip above the canvas.
- The interface looks like a desktop engineering application from 900 px: title bar and rail of sections in dark ink (with a line icon set shared by both apps), workspace of panels with title strips, document tabs, flat toolbar buttons, ruled dense tables, an ultramarine status bar. The account opens from the title bar. A phone keeps the top bar and bottom tabs. Same palette.
- New look of the web app and the console (shared `@epc/ui`): a technical drawing coloured by hand (warm paper, ink lines, flat washes of colour with a small offset, pencil hatching on progress bars), minimalist, light and dark. Mobile first: a top bar and bottom tabs on a phone, a side column from 900 px; 44 px touch targets, 16 px inputs, skip link, labelled navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, print without colour. All colours, shapes and sizes are tokens in `styles.css`.
- Plan: a circuit routed through plenums (nodes `PL`) adds the height (`altura_m`) of each plenum its route passes, once per circuit; the route reports `plenums` and `rise_m`. A plenum without a height is reported and adds nothing.
- Automatic groups of differentials: rules in the standard solutions put the circuits that have no differential of their own behind one, as many as are needed (circuits and current per differential, split by type and phase); switched off by default, edited in the console tab "Soluciones estándar". A change in the document of standard solutions now makes the memos of the boards stale. The multi-line draws the circuits behind a single-phase group on the group's phase.
- When the catalog of layers sets a line weight for a layer, the layer governs over the stroke widths of the SVG. The plan editor delivers its DXF through the deliverable service (a draft with roles) and its package has an index like the diagrams'.
- Standard solutions: the devices that make up each circuit are a document (`epc_calc/data/standard-solutions.json`) instead of three copies of code in the calculation, the single-line and the multi-line. The MASTER edits it in the console (tab "Soluciones estándar": conditions, priority and the chain of symbols with their poles and texts); a ready-made solution "lighting: differential + breaker" is there to switch on. The calculation delivers the chain of each circuit, the diagrams only draw it, and the differential sensitivity follows the project's criteria. Differences: motor heads follow the calculation's rule (miniature breaker up to 63 A, moulded-case above) in the drawings too, the variable-speed drive shows its tag in the single-line like in the multi-line, and a group's text in the table is the differential the drawing always showed.
- The title block is a service: the single-line and the multi-line ask `epc_titleblock` for their sheets with a JSON request instead of writing an Excel and starting processes; `epc_mld` no longer needs `epc_sld`. The three demo values the old template printed in every title block (type of drawing, "replaces" and internal code) are gone: they stay blank until the project gives them.
- Deliverable service (`epc_dxf`): the single-line and the multi-line hand over their SVG and get the DXF; SVG elements carry a semantic role (`data-layer`) that decides their layer, instead of their position on the sheet; every board design writes the index of its delivery package (`<name>_entregable.json`). The converters moved from `epc_titleblock` and `epc_sld` to `epc_dxf`. In the DXF the title block is now entirely in its layer and the table is annotation.
- The drawing engine is split into independent packages (`epc_symbols`, `epc_titleblock`, `epc_sld`, `epc_mld`, `epc_plan`, `epc_drawing`) with enforced layers (ADR-0022). Plugins are regenerated from the code: they declare only what they own, and `engine`, `requires` and Python packages are derived; new plugin `plan-drawing`. Skills the platform now does were removed. The dependency map is `docs/architecture/DEPENDENCIAS.md`.
- The single-line and multi-line diagrams only draw: every number they show (power, PE and neutral sections, main bar, feeder, checks, notes) comes from the calculation engine. The feeder shows "Pendiente" when there is no upstream board instead of an invented section.

### Added
- Self-contained modules: a contract (`docs/modules/MODULE-CONTRACT.md`), a generator (`tools/dev/new_module.py`) that creates a folder which runs, tests and demos on its own, and a prompt to start a focused chat. `incoming/` holds modules waiting to be integrated; `incoming/plan/` is the first one (the plan canvas, ADR-0020), with its specification started.
- Company registration (ADR-0017): the country decides the fields; legal name, tax identifier (CUIT, RIF, RFC, NIT, RUT or CIF, checked with its own rule), fiscal address, the company's e-mail, the administrator's alias (optional) and password, acceptance of the terms, and a 6-digit code sent to the company's e-mail. The registration must be made by a validated person of the corporation and the team may contact the company to validate it: the console shows what the company declared and the team validates or rejects it, and the company sees a notice while it is pending. A company no longer registers with the simple form or with a provider. E-mail delivery through `EPC_SMTP_*` (outbox folder in development); migration 0006.
- "View as" for the engineering team (and the development MASTER): from the organization selector, look at the platform as an organization's owner, administrator or member, or as an independent professional (an organization of one person). The server then applies exactly what that account may do and see, a banner says so, and going back is one click. Every use is recorded in the audit log (migration 0005, `sessions.view_as`).
- Organizations do their own engineering (ADR-0016): the owner, administrators and members of an organization (and an independent professional, an organization of one person) open Ingeniería and Datos for their own projects only. Clients, issuing companies, logos and personnel are per organization; personnel carry functions (designer, checker, approver, responsible, project manager, product owner, inspector, auditor) that the team pickers of a project use. Members read the organization's data, owners and administrators manage it.
- Issuing companies (Datos › Empresas emisoras): an engineer can issue documents in the name of several companies; each project picks one once (Ingeniería › Emisor y equipo), the default being the account's company. A project from before keeps its company and logo as the first issuer.
- Per-discipline team of a project (who designs, checks and approves each discipline, entered once per project). The title block of every board takes issuer, client and team from the project; a board keeps only its own description, location, scale, revision and status.
- Client filter in the project list; a client created with a project joins the client list, and a project whose client is not in the list offers to add it.
- Real route of a cable (Ingeniería › Unifilar › Recorrido, per circuit): tray, pass chamber, directly buried, buried duct, road crossing, exposed conduit, in any order, each with its length, temperatures, soil thermal resistivity, depth and circuits sharing it. The capacity of the cable is that of the most unfavourable stretch, the voltage drop uses the total length, the wiring inside the board keeps its own thermal section, and the memo lists the capacity of every stretch. The acometida of a board shows the route of the upstream circuit that feeds it. New design criteria: ambient temperature, soil temperature and soil thermal resistivity (`aea-calc` 1.8.0, `epc_calc.aea_route`).
- Several boards per project (Ingeniería › Unifilar): each board has its own data, calculation, memo, summary and diagrams; a board can be added at any time and declares which upstream board and circuit feeds it. Projects with the previous single board are migrated automatically.
- Calculation memo (MC) and project summary (SP) of every board, calculated before the diagram and issued as Markdown (view) and PDF (deliverable): acometida, internal wiring (thermal) vs outgoing wiring (voltage drop, with transition terminal flag), protections (curve, breaking capacity, minimum short-circuit current, overload coordination), residual-current device analysis, trip-curve bands and selectivity between protections in series. The diagram is generated only from a memo calculated on the current data.
- `aea-calc` 1.7.0 (`board_memo`), `board-design` 0.2.0 and patch bumps of the plugins that wrap the engine.

### Changed
- Engineering › Files shows calculations as a readable table (circuits, criteria, compliance) and Markdown as formatted text; the software's own formats (JSON, YAML, Python, Markdown download) are no longer shown to people (technical files only on request, administration).

## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-24
Third release: accounts and organizations with real sign-in (email, alias and Google, GitHub, Facebook, LinkedIn), roles and permissions, inspectors and auditors, master data, issuing to the client, criteria-driven calculation and multi-line diagrams, automatic analysis of customer input, management reports and registers of the methodology, norm review, and a single source of truth per datum.

### Fixed
- **Registers and Reports showed "could not connect to the API (409)" in some demo projects.** The demo generator gave projects a random methodology that included PMI, which does not exist yet (only PM² has a pack; a real project cannot be created with PMI), so their registers and report catalog were missing. The generator now uses PM² only, the 11 demo projects that had PMI were corrected, and a methodology without registers or reports is now reported as such (`available: false`, with a clear message on the screen) instead of failing.

### Added
- **Development MASTER account and account reset (ADR-0015).** A fixed account of the engineering team for development machines: `master` with the password set by `EPC_DEV_MASTER_PASSWORD` in the developer's own, git-ignored `local/dev.ps1` (the repository carries no credential; a new gate check `dev-credentials` fails if one is ever versioned), active only when `EPC_ENV=dev`, loopback only, hidden from every list, the audit log and the staff count, and impossible to register (`@dev.invalid` is reserved). New command `reset-accounts --yes [--audit]` (and `.\local\dev.ps1 reset-users`, with confirmation) that deletes every account, organization, session, invitation, assignment and linked identity (refused in production; projects on disk are kept). In development the sign-in screen now also shows the providers that have no keys yet (Google, GitHub, Facebook, LinkedIn) as disabled buttons marked *sin configurar*, so they can be seen before the keys exist; in production only configured providers are shown. `local/dev.ps1` loads the provider keys from `local/oauth.env`.
- **One expert account for the engineering team.** A single `staff` account now reaches every area without changing user: customer application (all projects of all organizations), technical console, norm review (new *Normas* entry in the customer application too), accounts, assignments, audit log, registers, reports, observations. It can also **work inside any organization** from the organization selector of the sidebar ("Toda la plataforma" by default): while inside one, the people and invitations page of that organization is available (it acts as its owner) and a project created from the application belongs to it. Both applications show a link to the other. Entering and leaving an organization, and everything done there, is recorded in the audit log under the staff member's own name. No other kind of account can do this. New `EPC_CONSOLE_URL` for the console link.
- **Sign in with an alias, and with Google, GitHub, Facebook and LinkedIn (ADR-0014, migration `0004`).** The sign-in form takes one box: email **or** username/alias (optional, chosen at sign-up, in the account page or by an invitation; unique, case-insensitive, no `@`). A provider button appears for each provider configured on the server (`EPC_OAUTH_<PROVIDER>_CLIENT_ID` / `_CLIENT_SECRET`, `EPC_SECRET_KEY`; step by step in `docs/oauth-providers.md`) and the same button signs in and registers: a new person picks independent or company and is created without a password (they can set one later); an invitation can be accepted with a provider that vouches for the invited email. Safety: OAuth code flow with PKCE and a signed state cookie; customer accounts only (staff, reviewers, inspectors and auditors always use their password); an existing account is linked automatically only when the provider vouches for the email (Facebook never does: link from *Account* after signing in); a provider account belongs to one person; the last way to sign in cannot be removed; everything goes to the audit log. Account page: alias, linked accounts and a password section that needs no current password for accounts created through a provider. Tested with simulated providers (no network); it has not been tried against the real services yet.
- **Account types, permission matrix, audit log, inspectors and auditors (ADR-0013, migration `0003`).** `/api/me` now reports eight kinds of account (`staff`, `reviewer`, `inspector`, `auditor`, `individual`, `corporate_owner`, `corporate_admin`, `corporate_member`) and the `abilities` of each; what each kind may do is one table (`identity/permissions.py`) enforced by the request guard, with a test that runs every kind against the same requests. New **company administrator** role (manages members and invitations, never an owner). New **inspector** and **external auditor** accounts, created by staff with the console, that see only the organizations assigned to them (`PUT /api/identity/users/{id}/assignments`, effective at once); the auditor is read-only. **Observations** (`/api/app/projects/{id}/observations`, a register of the methodology): inspectors, auditors and staff record them, the customer's owner or administrators answer, the recorder closes; new project tab *Observaciones*. Platform **audit log** (`GET /api/identity/audit`, console panel *Auditoría de la plataforma*): sign-ins, failed sign-ins, accounts, roles, invitations, assignments, password changes, organization switches and observations, never secrets. HTTP security headers on every response. Read-only accounts no longer see the buttons they cannot use (new project, send information, answer questions). A role change no longer ends the person's session (the role is read on every request).
- **One sign-in for every kind of account.** The person types only email and password; the system works out the type of account and `/api/me` says it (`account_type`): `staff` (engineering team), `reviewer` (norm reviewer), `individual` (independent user), `corporate_admin` (owner of a company subscription) or `corporate_member` (belongs to a corporation), plus the page where that account starts (`landing`: reviewers go straight to *Normas*, the others to their projects). The sidebar shows the type of account. A person who belongs to several organizations sees a selector and changes the active one without signing in again (`POST /api/auth/switch-org`; suspended organizations are not offered). A customer who opens the technical console is sent to the customer application.
- **One source of truth per datum.** New gate check `single-source` (`tools/ci/check_single_source.py`): it fails when two versioned files have the same content (test fixtures and three justified cases are exempt). First clean-up: the drawing engine no longer keeps a hand-written copy of the base design criteria (`BASE_CRITERIA` removed; `catalog_defaults()` reads `engine/jurisdictions/AR/electrical/design-criteria.json`, and a built plugin carries a copy *generated* from it through the new `engine_data` field of the plugin manifest, which also enters the plugin's hash so a corrected norm value marks the plugin as changed); the unused second library of multi-line symbols (`mld/data/mld-lv`, 78 files) and five copies of the same logo and one of the example workbook were deleted. The norm review screen now lives once in `@epc/ui` and is shown by the console and by the customer app for the reviewer role (it was only in the console).
- **Norm review (new module `norms`, new role `standards`):** a dedicated *norm reviewer* account that sees only the review of the norm data (the console shows just *Normas*; it reaches no projects, customers or technical console). By standard and edition the reviewer sees every point (177 rules, 29 calculation tables and constants, 15 base design criteria) and can **correct** it (rules: topic, text, type, parameters, scope, source; criteria: names, default, options, limits; tables: values only, never keys or sizes) and **approve** or **flag** it with a note. Corrections are written into the engine's data files with their own formatting; approvals (who, when, note, fingerprint of the value) go to `engine/jurisdictions/AR/review.json` and every action to `review-log.jsonl`; a corrected point returns to pending and a value changed after approval is *stale*. The criteria catalog is `validated` only when all 15 are approved, and the design pipeline now reads the catalog values (the engine's fixed copy is only a fallback). Staff create reviewer accounts in the console (*Organizaciones*) or with `create-admin --role standards`. `EPC_ROLE=standards` simulates it in development.
- **Project registers as platform data (new module `registers`, tab *Registros*):** risks (RIE), changes (RFC), issues (INC), decisions (DEC), assumptions (SUP) and stakeholders (STK) are no longer Markdown guides filled in by Claude sessions (a leftover of the previous framework) but validated records defined by the methodology pack (`management/methodologies/pm2/registers.json`: fields, options in Spanish and English, closing states). Risk level is derived (probability x impact; low <= 2, medium 3 to 16, high >= 20), closing stamps the date, nothing is deleted and every change keeps who, what and when. Endpoints `GET/POST /api/projects/{id}/registers/{kind}` and `PATCH .../{id}` (admin/engineer). The management reports D11 to D15 now read from them instead of parsing tables. The project skeleton drops `change-requests.md`, `issue-log.md`, `assumptions-and-decisions.md` and `stakeholders.md`, its `05-management/README.md` describes this platform's structure, and the D11 to D15 templates and the M8 catalog point to the platform (tabs *Informes* and *Registros*) instead of `90-management` and fixed phrases.
- **Management reports from the methodology (PM²):** the *Informes* tab now generates the reports the methodology asks for. The catalog comes from the methodology pack (`management/methodologies/pm2/reports.json`: D11 status, D12 progress, D13 risks, D14 effort, D15 changes) and each report follows its template of `rules/management-templates.md`, filled with the project's own data: status and milestones, WBS, schedule, backlog estimates, deliverables, requirements (open questions become the *decisions required*) and the PM² registers kept as Markdown tables (`change-requests.md`, `assumptions-and-decisions.md` RIE rows, `issue-log.md`, `stakeholders.md`; the illustrative `EJ-` rows are ignored). Overall and per-dimension status (green/amber/red) comes from stated rules and its trend compares with the previous report of the same type; what the platform does not hold is shown as *n/d* (hours actually worked are not recorded yet, so D14 reports earned hours from progress). Each report is saved as Markdown and PDF under `workspace/05-management/progress|reports/` (`STATUS-01_20261023.md`), stays internal and is published to the client's Reports list on demand (`POST /api/projects/{id}/reporting/{report}/publish`). Spanish and English. New engine exporter `epc_drawing.export.report_pdf`. The skill `create-management-document` remains for the documents that are not generated automatically.
- **Issuing: download package, transmittal document and sending message.** Each transmittal in *Emitir* has *Descargar paquete* (`GET .../issuing/{TR}/package`): a ZIP `<project>_<EMISSION>_<TR>_Rev<NN>.zip` with its files (folder structure kept), the **transmittal document** `TRANSMITTAL_<TR>_Rev<NN>.pdf` (a delivery note: project, client, issuer, emission, revisions, date, table of documents and formats, remarks, totals; no greetings; new engine exporter `epc_drawing.export.transmittal_pdf`) and `INDICE.csv` with SHA-256. *Mensaje de envío* shows the email text (subject and body, formal register: greeting, bulleted list of documents, package and integrity note, request for acknowledgement, closing and signature, in the project's language; templates in `backend/src/epc/modules/issuing/texts.py`) with copy buttons and *Abrir en mi correo*. Transmittals now record each item's discipline.
- **Answer open questions:** in Requirements each open question is clickable (*Responder*); the answer is stored with who answered and when (`POST /api/app/projects/{id}/requirements/{req}/answer`, field `answered_questions`), the question leaves the open list and the requirement becomes *Definido* when none is left. Answered questions are shown with author and date/time.
- **Accounts, organizations and tenant isolation (US-0018, ADR-0009):** real sign-in. `EPC_AUTH=simulated` (development default) keeps the role-from-environment mode; `EPC_AUTH=required` (production default) uses accounts stored in the database (migration `0002_identity`: passwords, platform role, seats, sessions, invitations; existing `admin`/`user` memberships become `owner`/`member`). **Individual** subscriptions (one person) and **corporate** ones (a group of people with `seats`); a customer sees only the projects of their organization (each project records its owner; another organization's project answers 404), the engineering staff see everything and the console. Opaque session tokens stored hashed (HttpOnly cookie or bearer), `scrypt` passwords, throttled sign-in, invitations (single-use link, 7 days, they hold a seat), owner screen *Cuenta* (people, roles, deactivate, invite, password) and console page *Organizaciones* (create organizations, seats, suspend, team accounts, set a temporary password, assign projects to organizations). CLI `python -m epc.modules.identity.cli create-admin` for the first staff account. Sign-up is closed unless `EPC_SIGNUP=open`. The compose stack now runs with accounts. Limits: no email delivery or reset by email, no MFA/SSO (see the ADR).
- **Automatic analysis of customer input (US-0016):** `POST /api/projects/{id}/analysis` (admin/engineer, explicit `confirm`) sends the pending items to the Claude API (text only, money amounts replaced, no attachments; forced `submit_analysis` tool call, model `EPC_ANALYSIS_MODEL`, default `claude-sonnet-5-5`), validates the answer with the plugin's own `intake.py validate` (one retry with the validator's feedback) and stores it with its `apply`, so it is identical to a manual run; nothing is written unless the whole analysis is valid; every call is recorded in `analysis_usage.json` (cost tracking, risk R-006). Web: *Analizar con Claude* in Input data for the admin/engineer. The key `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` stays on the server (`pip install epc-backend[analysis]`, included in the Docker image). Tested with a fake client (no network).
- **Criteria-driven design (plugin `board-design` 0.1.0):** the project's design criteria now **govern the calculation** and the calculation **feeds the diagrams**. `epc_calc.aea_pipeline.calculate` takes `criterios` (ΔU limits per circuit type, minimum sections, conductor material and insulation with B2 for XLPE, RCD sensitivity) and a `detalle` dict (type, limit, own and accumulated ΔU, Iz, compliance); the SLD generator takes that result (`SLD_CALC_JSON`, `SLD_CALC_DETAIL`, `SLD_LIM_*`) instead of its simplified calculation and prints the limits it was given. New `epc_drawing.board_design` (CLI and module: calculation -> SLD -> MLD, `--project` or `--input/--criteria`), skill `design-board`, and the web table *Cálculo de los circuitos*. The earlier notice that the criteria were not read is gone.
- **Multi-line diagram generator (US-0019, plugin `mld` 0.2.0, no longer pending):** `epc_drawing.mld.mld_generator` expands each line of the board into its conductors (L1 L2 L3 N) with the MLD_LV symbols per pole (10 mm pitch, not scaled), per-phase balancing of single-phase loads, RCCB groups as sub-busbars, PE, external cables, pagination in IRAM sheets (SVG and DXF); web button *Generar multifilar*; `convert-sld-to-mld` documents the automatic path. The plugin `sld` is 0.1.2.
- **Issuing step (Engineering -> Deliverables):** backend module `issuing` and *Emitir* sub-tab. Prepares a transmittal `TR-nnn` with stamped copies of the DXF (`epc_drawing.export.stamp`: client revision, internal revision, transmittal, hash, engine and plugin versions in the drawing header; the master is untouched), the PDF (existing or rendered from the stamped DXF) and workbooks under `deliverables/<emission>/TR-nnn_Rev<NN>/<discipline>/`; registers them as in review and writes `deliverables/transmittals/TR-nnn.json` plus a ledger event; *Confirm sent* turns them into delivered so the customer sees them and the ZIP package includes them. Only formats with `deliver: true` in the policy are issued.
- **Datos (master data):** new area for what does not change between projects, entered once: company profile, clients, people and logos (backend module `masterdata`, files in `EPC_DATA`, web page *Datos* for the admin/engineer role). Saved clients can be picked when creating a project; the SLD titleblock is prefilled from them and the logos reach the drawing (the titleblock glue now also copies PNG logos).
